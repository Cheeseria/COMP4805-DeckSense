### Primer for the Blue-Red Control and Combo Deck

This deck is designed to leverage card advantage, efficient spellcasting, and powerful synergies to overwhelm opponents. The core of the deck revolves around generating card advantage and mana efficiently, while maintaining control over the game through counterspells and cost reduction. Here’s a detailed breakdown of the key engines and how they loop together.

#### Engine 1: Card Draw and Mana Generation

**Key Cards:**
- **The Celestus**
- **Inner Fire**
- **Ashling, Flame Dancer**

**Refueling Cards:**
- **The Celestus**
- **Ashling, Flame Dancer**

**Progression:**
1. **The Celestus** provides card draw and mana. It allows you to draw a card and add {U} or {R} to your mana pool.
2. Use the mana generated by **The Celestus** to cast **Inner Fire**, which adds {R} to your mana pool.
3. With the additional mana from **Inner Fire**, cast **Ashling, Flame Dancer**. This card provides card draw and deals damage, allowing you to continue drawing cards and generating more mana.
4. The card draw from **Ashling, Flame Dancer** can be used to recast **The Celestus**, restarting the loop and maintaining a steady flow of cards and mana.

#### Engine 2: Cost Reduction and Counterspelling

**Key Cards:**
- **Goblin Electromancer**
- **Mana Drain**
- **Negate**

**Refueling Cards:**
- **Goblin Electromancer**

**Progression:**
1. **Goblin Electromancer** reduces the cost of spells, allowing you to cast **Mana Drain** and **Negate** more efficiently.
2. **Mana Drain** can be used to gain life and counter spells, while **Negate** counters noncreature spells.
3. These counterspells protect your resources, allowing you to continue reducing costs and maintaining control over the game.
4. The cost reduction provided by **Goblin Electromancer** can be used to recast itself, restarting the loop and maintaining control over the game.

#### Engine 3: Spell Copy and Card Draw

**Key Cards:**
- **Reiterate**
- **Narset's Reversal**

**Refueling Cards:**
- **Reiterate**

**Progression:**
1. **Reiterate** allows you to copy an instant or sorcery spell and return it to your hand, providing card draw and spell redundancy.
2. **Narset's Reversal** copies an instant or sorcery spell and returns it to its owner's hand, allowing you to maintain control over the game.
3. The card draw from **Reiterate** can be used to recast **Reiterate** or **Narset's Reversal**, restarting the loop and maintaining control over the game.

#### Engine 4: Storm and Card Advantage

**Key Cards:**
- **Grapeshot**
- **Brain Freeze**

**Refueling Cards:**
- **Grapeshot**
- **Brain Freeze**

**Progression:**
1. **Grapeshot** deals damage and has the Storm ability, which allows you to copy the spell for each spell cast before it this turn.
2. **Brain Freeze** mills cards and also has the Storm ability, allowing you to copy the spell for each spell cast before it this turn.
3. The copies of **Grapeshot** and **Brain Freeze** can be used to deal damage and mill cards, respectively, while also generating additional copies of themselves.
4. The card draw and mill effects can be used to recast **Grapeshot** and **Brain Freeze**, restarting the loop and maintaining control over the game.

#### Win Conditions

1. **Card Advantage and Mana Efficiency:**
   - **The Celestus**, **Inner Fire**, and **Ashling, Flame Dancer** provide a steady flow of card draw and mana, allowing you to overwhelm opponents with powerful spells and maintain control over the game.
   - **Goblin Electromancer**, **Mana Drain**, and **Negate** provide cost reduction and counterspelling, allowing you to protect your resources and maintain control over the game.
   - **Reiterate** and **Narset's Reversal** provide spell copying and card draw, allowing you to maintain control over the game and generate additional card advantage.
   - **Grapeshot** and **Brain Freeze** provide damage and mill effects, respectively, while also generating additional copies of themselves through the Storm ability.

By leveraging these engines and win conditions, you can maintain a steady flow of card draw and mana, while also protecting your resources and maintaining control over the game. This allows you to overwhelm opponents with powerful spells and maintain control over the game, ultimately leading to victory.
